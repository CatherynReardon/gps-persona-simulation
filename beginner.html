This page is designed for first-time undergraduate researchers. Move through steps 1-5, write short responses, and use the final Canvas draft as your assignment response.

  2. Make a first prediction before looking closely.
  3. Use the trait data as evidence.
  4. Test your idea in one decision scenario.
  5. Explain what the data helped you understand and what it cannot prove.
