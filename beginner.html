Before You Begin

Your Research Mission

You are not trying to diagnose or predict a real person. You are practicing how psychologists use data as evidence while also noticing uncertainty, individual differences, and bias.

Learning goal

Explain how one behavioral trait may shape a social decision.

Evidence to use

Use at least one GPS trait score and the simulated scenario result.
