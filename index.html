Guided Psychology Study Simulation

Move through the steps of a psychology study: ask, predict, analyze, observe, interpret, and evaluate limits.

Student Research Steps
1. Ask

Choose one research question for the simulation trial.

2. Predict

Write a hypothesis before interpreting the outcome.

3. Analyze

Use trait data as psychological evidence.

4. Observe

Run the simulated trial and record the choice.

5. Interpret

Explain whether the evidence supports or challenges the hypothesis.

6. Evaluate

Discuss how evidence, bias, and individual variation shaped the interpretation.

Teacher note

This is a classroom simulation for learning about bias, interpretation, and research design. Students should not export data from this activity. Any formal human-subjects study or instructor-led data collection would need institutional approval before collecting identifiable or sensitive data.
