What students do in this lab

Look for the strongest GPS traits. Treat them as clues, not facts about all people.

4. Reflect on bias

Ask what assumption you made and how the model could be misused.

Student Research Activity

Use a short trial study to see how information changes student judgments.

Student Research Steps
1. Set up

Use an anonymous classroom ID and choose one research question.

2. Begin

The app creates a trial and decides what information the participant sees.

3. Record

Choose a response, rate confidence and trust, and write one sentence explaining why.

4. Repeat

Run several trials so students can compare patterns across conditions.

5. Debrief

Discuss what changed when country labels, traits, or behavior were shown or hidden.

6. Export

Download the CSV and analyze class responses without using names.

Teacher note

This is a classroom simulation for learning about bias, interpretation, and research design. A formal human-subjects study would need institutional approval before collecting identifiable or sensitive data.

Study Setup

Enter an anonymous ID, choose a question, then click Begin Study. The app will create Trial 1.

Ethics note

Personas are simulated from survey data. They do not represent all people from a country. Avoid collecting names or sensitive identifiers unless your institution has approved that protocol.
